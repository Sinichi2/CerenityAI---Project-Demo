# Safety and crisis support

Cerenity is a scheduled-care service. It is not an emergency service and nobody is monitoring the
app around the clock. If you are in danger right now, use the routes below rather than waiting for
a session or a reply.

## If you are in crisis right now

In the United States, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. It is free,
confidential and open 24 hours. You can also text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line. If
someone's life is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Outside the US, contact your local emergency number. The International Association for Suicide
Prevention maintains a directory of crisis centres by country at iasp.info/crisis-centres.

## What the Cerenity assistant will and will not do

The assistant answers questions about plans, practitioners, policies and how the service works. It
does not diagnose, does not give clinical advice, and does not provide crisis counselling. If you
tell it you are thinking about suicide, self-harm, or that you are being hurt by someone, it will
stop what it was doing, say plainly that it is not an emergency service, and give you the routes
above. That is the correct behaviour, not a failure.

## Between sessions

Members on Plus and Household can message their practitioner in the app. Practitioners reply within
one business day. Messaging is not monitored overnight or at weekends, so it is not a route for
urgent help.

## Safety planning

If you have had thoughts of suicide or self-harm, ask your practitioner to build a written safety
plan with you in your next session. A safety plan lists your warning signs, the coping steps that
have worked before, the people you can call, and the places you can go. It lives in your account and
you can open it without signing in to anything else.

## Reporting a concern about a practitioner

Email conduct@cerenity.example or use the Report option on any session in your history. Reports go
to the clinical director, not to your practitioner. We acknowledge within one business day and
respond with an outcome within ten. You can switch practitioners immediately while a report is open,
and doing so does not affect your session allowance.
