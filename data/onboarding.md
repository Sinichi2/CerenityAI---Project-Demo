# Getting started and matching

New members go through a short intake, get matched with a practitioner, and book a first session.
Most people finish the whole thing in under a week.

## The intake questionnaire

The intake takes about 12 minutes. It asks what brought you here, what you have tried before, your
availability, your preferred language, and whether you have a preference about your practitioner's
gender or background. You can skip any question. Nothing in the intake is shared outside the
matching team and the practitioner you are matched with.

## How matching works

A member of the clinical matching team reads your intake and picks a practitioner whose specialty,
modality and availability fit what you described. It is a person making the decision, not an
algorithm scoring you. You get a short written note explaining why that practitioner was chosen.

How long it takes depends on your plan. The matching promise is 96 hours on Core, 48 hours on Plus
and 24 hours on Household. If we miss it, that month is free.

## Your first session

First sessions are 50 minutes like any other. The practitioner will spend the first half asking
about your history and what you want out of this, and the second half proposing a rough shape for
the work: how often you will meet, what approach they plan to use, and what you should expect to
feel different about in three months. You do not have to commit to anything in the first session.

## Switching practitioners

You can switch at any time, for any reason, without explaining yourself. Use Settings, Care team,
Request a change. The matching team offers a new practitioner within your plan's matching window.
Switching does not use up a session, and unused sessions carry over during the gap.

When you switch you choose whether to pass your session history to the new practitioner or start
clean. Most people pass it on, which saves repeating the intake story, but it is genuinely your
call and the new practitioner is not told which option you picked.

## Rescheduling and no-shows

Reschedule from the session card up to 24 hours before the start time and the session returns to
your allowance. Inside 24 hours, or if you do not show, the session is counted as used. Practitioners
wait 15 minutes before marking a session as a no-show. If a practitioner cancels on you, the session
is returned and you get a credit for one extra session that month.
