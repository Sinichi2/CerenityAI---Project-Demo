# Billing, pausing and cancellation

Memberships bill monthly on the date you joined. Prices are in US dollars and include the sessions
listed on your plan. There is no joining fee and no charge for the intake.

## Changing plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged the difference for the remainder of the month.
Downgrades take effect at your next billing date, so you keep the higher allowance until then.
Changing plan never changes your practitioner, but dropping to Core removes messaging access and
some practitioners are not bookable on Core.

## Unused sessions

Sessions do not roll over from month to month, with one exception: if the matching team missed your
plan's matching promise, or your practitioner cancelled on you, those sessions carry into the next
month. The carried balance shows on your account under Sessions, Carried forward.

## Pausing

You can pause a membership for up to three months once per year. Billing stops, your practitioner is
held for you, and your session history stays intact. Pause from Settings, Membership, Pause. If you
need longer than three months, cancel instead: we keep your matching notes for a year so restarting
does not mean redoing intake.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time from Settings, Membership. There is no notice period and no cancellation fee.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month and you keep access to sessions
and messaging until then. We do not pro-rate partial months.

## Refunds

We refund the current month in full if you cancel within seven days of your first ever session, no
questions asked. Outside that window, refunds are handled case by case by support. Sessions that
were used are not refunded.

## Insurance, HSA and FSA

Cerenity does not bill insurers directly. You can request a superbill from Settings, Billing,
Superbill, and submit it to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement. It includes the
practitioner's licence number, the CPT codes and the diagnosis code, which are what most insurers
ask for. Cerenity memberships are generally eligible for HSA and FSA cards, and most members pay
that way. Check with your plan administrator, because eligibility depends on your employer's setup
rather than on us.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails we retry after three days and again after seven. Your account stays active
through both retries. Sessions already booked are not cancelled. If the third attempt fails the
membership pauses rather than cancels, so nothing is lost when you update the card.
